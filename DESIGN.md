# Henle Printing design direction — “Midwest Colorworks”

Henle is a long-established craft business with modern production capability. The visual system therefore borrows from the working language of a pressroom—process colors, registration marks, stacked sheets, trim notches, and tactile stock—without turning the site into a nostalgic printer’s specimen book.

## Palette

- Ink Navy — `#102A43`
- Press Cyan — `#00A6C8`
- Warm Magenta — `#F15A4A`
- Harvest Gold — `#F2B134`
- Coated White — `#F7F6F1`
- Black Ink — `#121820`

## Type

- Display: Fraunces, a sturdy optical serif with enough personality to suggest craft and permanence.
- Body/UI: Manrope, a precise geometric sans that keeps forms and service information clear and current.

## Layout and signature

The page is composed like a press sheet: asymmetric fields, cropped color bars, overlapping paper cards, generous quiet areas, and deliberate diagonal movement. Oversized CMYK registration targets are the signature device, used sparingly as both brand punctuation and a subtle motion motif.

## Generic-default check

The initial temptation was a restrained heritage palette and conventional full-width sections. That felt interchangeable with a coffee roaster or architecture studio. The revised direction uses bright process color, hard paper edges, print-specific language, and tightly framed physical work—visual choices that could only belong to a printer.
